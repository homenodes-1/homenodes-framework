# HomeNodes Roadmap

**Last updated:** 2026-09-25

Target dates are planning estimates. Phase 2 onward depends on funding and Phase 1 results.

## Overview

| Phase | Focus | Target dates | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proof of concept and draft framework | Oct 2026 to Jan 2027 | In progress |
| 2 | City expansion (5 to 10 homes) | Feb 2027 to Jun 2027 | Planned |
| 3 | Governance framework v1.0 | Jun 2027 to Sep 2027 | Planned |
| 4 | Publication and policy engagement | Sep 2027 to Dec 2027 | Planned |

## Phase 1: Proof of concept and draft framework

**Goal:** A single operating residential node, and a draft framework informed by it within 12 weeks.

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Repositories, framework outline, gap analysis, literature review (v0.1) | Sep 2026 (complete) |
| Hardware procured | Oct 2026 |
| Node built, hardened, and isolated on the home network | Oct 2026 |
| Energy monitoring live, pre-launch baseline recorded | Oct 2026 |
| Node listed on Vast.ai | Nov 2026 |
| Expert interviews (compute governance, platform operators, privacy) | Oct to Nov 2026 |
| Gap analysis v0.2, validated against node operation | Nov 2026 |
| Draft framework v0.5, all five domains with draft provisions | Dec 2026 |
| Eight weeks of operating and energy data published | Jan 2027 |

**Outputs:** Draft framework v0.5, public energy dataset, documented build and security configuration.

## Phase 2: City expansion

**Goal:** Test the draft framework across different households and energy profiles in Medicine Hat.

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Research protocol and participant consent process | Feb 2027 |
| Recruit 5 to 10 households (gas-primary, solar-supplemented, grid-primary) | Mar 2027 |
| Nodes operating under protocol | Apr to Jun 2027 |
| Engagement with the City of Medicine Hat utility and planning departments | Ongoing |

**Outputs:** Multi-home energy and operations data, framework revisions based on real operator experience.

## Phase 3: Governance framework v1.0

**Goal:** A complete, publicly available framework.

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Framework v0.9 released for public comment | Jul 2027 |
| Comment period and revisions | Jul to Aug 2027 |
| Framework v1.0 published | Sep 2027 |

**Outputs:** HomeNodes Governance Framework v1.0, licensed CC BY 4.0.

## Phase 4: Publication and policy engagement

**Goal:** Get the framework in front of the people who can use it.

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Preprint submitted | Sep 2027 |
| Conference or workshop presentation | Fall 2027 |
| Briefings for municipal, provincial, and federal policymakers | Fall 2027 |
| Engagement with compute governance researchers and platform operators | Ongoing |

**Outputs:** Preprint, presentation materials, policy briefs.
